# Poke

Poke is a personal accountability system. You setup recurring goals on the platform and get reminded about them via text message. For example, if you want to go to the gym three days a week, you can add a reminder in Poke to message you on your gym days. Poke will keep track of when you accomplish your goals or when you've slipped up.

## MVP

- User can create reminders that will be regularly sent as text
- User can set which days reminders are sent
- User can set which times reminders are sent
- User can respond to text to confirm completion
- User can response to text to disable reminders (”snooze”)
- User gets reminded if they do not respond to text
- User can manage their reminders

## Stretch

- User can see dashboard summarizing their reminders
- User can see data visualization of their streaks

## Physics of Accountability

Just like Newton's laws govern physical motion, similar principles apply to building habits:

### First Law: Inertia
**An object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion.**

Breaking through initial resistance is the hardest part. That's where Poke comes in - providing the external force (reminder) needed to overcome inertia and get you started. Once you're in motion, maintaining momentum becomes easier.

### Second Law: Force = Mass × Acceleration
**The acceleration of an object depends on the force applied and its mass.**

Small, consistent forces (daily reminders) applied over time create significant acceleration toward your goals. Poke applies these regular forces to help you build momentum, even when the "mass" of your responsibilities feels heavy.

### Third Law: Action-Reaction
**For every action, there is an equal and opposite reaction.**

Every time you respond to a Poke reminder and complete your goal, you create positive momentum. Skip a day, and you feel the opposite reaction - lost progress and motivation. Poke helps you maintain the action-reaction cycle in the positive direction.

### Kinetic Energy: ½mv²
**Moving objects have energy proportional to their velocity squared.**

The faster you build your streak (velocity), the more energy and motivation you accumulate. Poke tracks your streaks to help you visualize this exponential growth in momentum.

### Conservation of Momentum
**In a closed system, momentum is conserved.**

Your accountability system works best when it's consistent. Poke ensures the system stays closed and your momentum is conserved through regular check-ins and tracking.
